Which plausible worlds lose this power book 25% of its capital?

A reverse stress testing agent for a NYISO book: Claude proposes coherent scenarios as typed shock vectors, a factor engine prices them, history judges their plausibility.

Handout topic 6, Reverse Stress Testing Agent · solo project · November 2026

github.com/tchimby/nyiso-reverse-stress

Conventional stress testing starts from a scenario and asks what we lose. This project does the reverse: it starts from the loss, 5 million dollars on a 20 million book, and asks which worlds get us there and how plausible they are. The LLM proposes the worlds; code prices them and judges them.
