Keeping the LLM from fantasy

Five guards in code, and every call on the record

1. Schema with ranges

Eight keys, each bounded by history. Anything else is rejected before the engine.

2. Hard rules

A shock that contradicts its own narrative, or a duration past 30 days, fails the gate.

3. Analogue table

Four verified events to cite; a cited analogue that is not in the table is flagged as fabricated.

4. Marginal ceilings

No single shock beyond 1.25× its historical extreme; a $5,000/MWh price cap.

5. Soft penalty

Reusing the same driver family costs score, so the search is pushed to cover more worlds.

Audit log

Prompt version and hash, messages in and out, model, cache key, engine verdicts. Every scenario is reproducible from its line.

Final run: 0 of 177 scenarios rejected by a hard rule; the driver-overuse penalty fired on 64-71% of proposals. The agent concentrates on cold snaps once it finds they work.

At the end: a different model, Opus 5.5, grades each ranked scenario blind on five coherence items; a human can do the same in the demo.
