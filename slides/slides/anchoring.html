Model-dependent

Haiku’s severity follows the stated target; without it the proposals never reach the tail. Sonnet without a target proposes harsher worlds, and fewer count as breaches because more fall past the plausibility limits.

The two arms are not the same task (a goal versus no goal), so this shows dependence on the target, not that the scenarios are invented. For Sonnet the target mainly restrains severity.
