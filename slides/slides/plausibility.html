Plausibility

History, not the model, says how plausible a world is

Five daily factors

  • g log Henry Hub gas
  • l log peak load versus seasonal normal
  • s WEST price residual after gas, load and season
  • c N.Y.C. minus WEST on-peak spread
  • d log real-time over day-ahead

Mean and covariance per season: winter 632 days, summer 644, shoulder 1,281.

Distance and rank

A scenario’s 10-day average factor deviation gives a Mahalanobis D².

q is its rank among the 255 non-overlapping 10-day windows of 2019-2025. A rank, not a probability.

Classes: ordinary q > 5%, adverse 1-5%, severe < 1%. Implausible if a marginal shock exceeds 1.25× the historical extreme or a hard rule fails.

What counts as a breach

A distinct scenario with

  • loss ≥ $5M
  • q < 5%
  • all hard rules passed
  • not implausible

Breaches per repeat is the headline metric; median D² is the plausibility metric.
