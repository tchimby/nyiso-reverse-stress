Honest reading

Where the agent adds value, and where it does not

Plausibility: no edge

As predicted, the optimizer and the conditional-Gaussian search find more plausible worlds on every engine version. Claude’s median D² is 1.7× the optimizer’s.

Breach count: real in this draw, not comparable

11.2 vs 8.0 rests on one seed, fewer engine calls, and a book not re-sized for the final engine. Per call Claude yields more; budgets are not equal.

Sizing the book: a negative result

A pre-declared rule (5 breaches, 2 channels, q < 5%) was tried on 48 + 432 candidate books. None is admissible: every breach is the winter N.Y.C. load-obligation story.

Coverage and readability: the edge

Driver coverage 0.34 vs 0.20. Scenarios a risk manager can read, check against a named analogue, trace position by position, and reject. Every proposal reproducible from its prompt hash.

Not a machine for finding the most plausible loss, which an optimizer does better. A machine for turning a loss target into worlds a human can argue with.
