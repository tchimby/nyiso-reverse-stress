One scenario, as Claude returns it

{"gas_hh_pct": 20, "ny_gas_basis_usd": 8, "load_peak_ratio": 1.03, "pocket_minus_ref_usd": 300, "dart_pct": 120, "duration_days": 7}

plus a narrative, an analogue (Winter Storm Elliott, Dec 2022), a confidence and its own loss estimate ($7.0M; engine: $7.22M)

The engine applies the shock for the stated days, then lets price-level and spread effects decay as φ^k with φ ≈ 0.55 and 0.59, the persistence measured in history. P&L is shocked path minus unshocked path.
